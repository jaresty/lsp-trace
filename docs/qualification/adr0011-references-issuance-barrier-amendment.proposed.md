# ADR 0011 — proposed references issuance-barrier amendment

**Status: PROPOSED / NOT ACCEPTED FOR IMPLEMENTATION / NOT QUALIFIED.** This is a narrow proposed amendment to the independently accepted `REFERENCES_SYMBOL_V1` implementation contract recorded in `adr0011-references-contract-implementation-acceptance.md` (`ed91b585`; contract SHA-256 `d71ec03c9244eb70fe9d93023410e0dc5cedc9932c992b7ff1ef45f2fc42f7f9`; matrix SHA-256 `c370fd6ae76bb3b74a15da12a7a9c41d9730c68662830294a50dfe89177546a2`). It does not revise those accepted bytes in place. An independent maintainer must explicitly accept or reject this delta before implementation adopts its new record meanings. The historical omitted-selector `CALLS_ONLY` path and all other ADR 0011 gates remain unchanged.

## Contradiction to resolve

The test-owned checkpoint currently computes `T=1,A=P` from complete-result evaluation and publishes an immutable terminal before an occurrence ledger. On occurrence publication or readback failure, this leaves terminal bytes claiming admission even though matrix rows 35–36 require no newly **issued** terminal (`T=0`) or occurrence (`A=0`). Its publication helper also loses the exact selector/digest on committed verification failure. There is **no reciprocal selector/digest cycle** in the current records: the terminal names method/target; the occurrence ledger names the terminal. The problem is the issuance boundary, not a hash cycle. A persisted but unverified artifact is never an issued receipt.

## Proposed acyclic roles and authority

1. A separately replay-verified subordinate document-symbol target and a matched, independently verified references method observation remain prerequisites. Manager-owned request write/read observations are not evaluator events. They do not authenticate a producer.
2. A private, immutable **evaluation proposal** binds the exact target/method receipts, keyed complete raw-result digest, policy, independently recorded ordinal begin/terminal evaluation events, observed `B`, countable `E`, `E_B/E_T`, successful whole-result `P`, and proposed closed outcome/disposition. It has no issued `T` or `A`; malformed results still have whole-result `P=0`, with any diagnostic prefix segregated. Neither parsed-item length nor a self-consistent proposal alone measures `B` or element transitions.
3. A private, immutable **inert occurrence candidate** binds the verified evaluation proposal, method and target, every original ordinal/range/identity and proposed cardinality. It has **no admitted `A`**, is never an eligible typed input, and cannot be replay-promoted by a caller. `null` and `[]` remain distinct retained raw results even when both propose `P=0`. A malformed, limited, or withheld result cannot produce an admission candidate; its mandatory diagnostic arm remains separate.
4. A private, immutable **final issuance receipt** refers backward to the exact verified candidate and proposal selectors/digests. Independent replay checks their expected identities, ordinal multiplicity, method/target source and revision custody, policies, canonical bytes, and all bounded counts before the final receipt is verified. Only after its no-replace publication **and verified readback** may the owner return a composite issuing receipt with one terminal member (`T=1`) and newly admitted occurrences (`A=P`, or zero for eligible `COMPLETE_EMPTY`). The final receipt is the issued terminal authority. Each admitted occurrence binds its proposal, method and target directly and the issued terminal by the verified final→candidate composition; **the stakeholder selected this composite binding in principle; the exact versioned delta still requires independent implementation-contract acceptance**. No public/root-selected issuance follows from this internal test-owned path.
5. Replay of an issued occurrence must start with the exact final receipt and traverse final→candidate→proposal→method/target, with independent expectations at every boundary. It cannot treat an inert candidate, proposal, or failed final publication as a terminal or occurrence receipt. In particular, a substituted or absent final receipt invalidates the entire candidate and every ordinal despite intact method, target and ranges. Occurrence identity must include the original ordinal and transaction-bound target/method identity; candidate selection by caller-supplied selector alone cannot confer admission. Retention transfers from transaction ownership to the admitted ledger only after final verification, with an immutable exact dependency list. Failed/intermediate objects remain private and inactive under a specified bounded transaction-retention policy; deletion, privacy revocation, dependent tombstones and unexplained missing bytes keep the accepted fail-closed lifecycle semantics.

## Proposed closed success-arm wire contract (decision candidate, not an accepted schema)

Assumption: the accepted target and method artifacts retain their own frozen formats; this proposal does not rename or silently reserialize them. The three **new** canonical JSON roles are `REFERENCES_EVALUATION_PROPOSAL_V1`, `REFERENCES_OCCURRENCE_CANDIDATE_V1`, and `REFERENCES_FINAL_ISSUANCE_V1`, each with a distinct literal `schema_version` equal to its role name and a distinct domain separator of the same value in every identity derivation. Their v1 formats are **proposed**, not implementation-authorizing schemas. Canonical serialization means strict UTF-8 JSON, sorted object keys, no duplicate keys (including decoded aliases), no unknown fields, no trailing data, integers only for counters/ordinals, and exact bytes re-encoded identically on readback. An artifact digest is lowercase `sha256:` plus 64 lowercase hexadecimal digits of the complete canonical bytes; the digest field is never embedded in its own hashed bytes. Each artifact is immutable, 0600, single-link, under the owner-selected 0700 root, with atomic no-replace publication and exact-byte readback. The owner derives a relative selector `adr0011-references-issuance-v1-<role>-<64-hex>.json` from the digest (role is respectively `proposal`, `candidate`, `final`); no caller-supplied path is admissible. Selection is the pair `(selector,digest)`, and the owner independently recomputes both. Identical bytes cannot substitute across roles because both role and domain are bound. This selector syntax is a **proposed choice**, not a claim that existing method/target selector formats change.

A `Ref` is an exact closed tuple `{selector,digest,schema_version}`; a `Context` is `{transaction_id,query_occurrence_id,method_ref,target_ref,source_identity_ref,revision_identity_ref,method_policy_ref,admission_policy_ref,privacy_policy_ref,retention_policy_ref,implementation_digest,schema_digests,session_id,generation,request_key,invocation_id}`. Source and revision identities are **explicit references to independently pinned predecessor records**, not free-form identity strings: the source record must bind exact normalized prepared URI, version, digest and position encoding; the revision record must bind root/commit/custody and both host Git observations without upgrading caller custody. Method, admission, privacy and transaction-retention policies likewise require separately pinned refs. No predecessor role is considered pinned by the proposed ref syntax alone; schema IDs, exact selector grammar, versions and canonical bytes remain independent acceptance blockers. `transaction_id = sha256(domain || canonical(Context without transaction_id))`; reject any context whose recomputed ID differs, or whose method/target independently replayed identity differs. Policy and schema digest arrays are closed, sorted by role, unique, and equality-checked against owner-selected expectations, not merely checked for internal consistency. The exact predecessor grammars and accepted digest IDs remain **blockers** until pinned; the draft schema closes `Context` at its own level and represents independently pinned identity records as exact refs rather than pretending to validate their unpinned nested bodies.

The proposal's closed fields are `{schema_version,context,raw_result_ref,raw_result_presence,raw_result_digest,raw_result_byte_length,scanner_observation_ref,top_level_form,declared_n,evaluator_event_ref,observed_b,known_e,observed_e_b,observed_e_t,whole_result_p,proposed_outcome,proposed_disposition}`. `top_level_form` is `NULL|ARRAY`; `raw_result_presence` must be `PRESENT`, with exact `null` and `[]` distinct by raw-result digest; `declared_n=1`, `observed_b=1`, `known_e=observed_e_b=observed_e_t=whole_result_p`, `known_e≤1000`, and proposed outcome/disposition is `COMPLETE/ITEMS` iff E>0, otherwise `COMPLETE_EMPTY/EMPTY`. `raw_result_ref` points to a separately verified bounded retained raw-result object and cannot be replaced by the proposal's copy of its digest. The proposal records **no issued terminal, admitted A, or independently verified method outcome**. An event-ref failure means no success proposal, even if a parser produced a plausible item list.

The candidate's closed fields are `{schema_version,context,proposal_ref,occurrences,proposed_p}`. `occurrences` is an ordered array of exactly `proposed_p=whole_result_p` records `{ordinal,returned_uri,returned_range,occurrence_id}` with ordinals exactly `0..P−1`; retain duplicates by ordinal, without deduplication or sorting by range. Proposed occurrence identity is `sha256("REFERENCES_OCCURRENCE_V1" || canonical(transaction_id,query_occurrence_id,method_ref,target_ref,ordinal,returned_uri,returned_range))`. Verify each range's exact encoding and source supply independently against the raw result at that ordinal; reject mismatch rather than normalizing equal locations. No candidate record has `admitted`, `terminal_ref`, `authority`, or an issued outcome; it is inert even if all its bytes verify.

The final receipt's closed fields are `{schema_version,context,proposal_ref,candidate_ref,issued_outcome,issued_disposition,n,b,t,e,e_b,e_t,p,a,dependency_refs}`. For the success arm `n=b=t=1`, `e=e_b=e_t=p=a=proposed_p`; `issued_outcome/disposition` matches the proposal's closed pair. `dependency_refs` is a sorted unique immutable exact list of proposal, candidate, method, target, raw result, evaluator-event, source and policy objects (including transitive source dependencies), with no self-reference to final. The final receipt is the **only new terminal**. A final receipt's expected digest and selector are computed from the complete canonical final bytes, separately from the candidate digest. A caller receives a composite `(final_ref, verified terminal, ordinal occurrence refs)` only after final no-replace publication, byte-exact readback and full independent replay; an occurrence reference is `(final_ref,ordinal,occurrence_id)`, not a candidate selector. Replay entrypoint is `ReplayIssuedReferences(final_ref, owner_expected_context, owner_expected_policy_and_schema_digests)`: recompute final selector/digest, traverse final→candidate→proposal→method/target/raw/event/source/policies, compare independently supplied expectations at each edge and recompute every ordinal ID and count. It returns all-or-nothing; no caller-supplied proposal/candidate selector can initiate issuance. This is the requested **indirect** terminal binding; the accepted contract's direct terminal binding cannot be considered met until a maintainer accepts this change explicitly.

### Evaluator event source (distinct from parsed-item counts)

A separate append-only owner-captured keyed event object, referenced by `evaluator_event_ref`, records `{schema_version:REFERENCES_EVALUATOR_EVENTS_V1,transaction_id,request_key,invocation_id,response_read_ref,events}`. Each event is `{sequence,kind,ordinal}` with strictly consecutive sequence and `ordinal=null` for `QUERY_BEGIN`, `QUERY_TERMINAL`; the valid success sequence is `QUERY_BEGIN`, then for each ordinal in order `ELEMENT_BEGIN(i),ELEMENT_TERMINAL(i,VALID_PENDING_ADMISSION)`, then `QUERY_TERMINAL(ITEMS|EMPTY)`. Each event is emitted at the evaluator transition, **before** the evaluator advances to the next state, by an owner-controlled sink bound to the selected keyed response-read observation; a terminal event cannot be emitted without its preceding begin or if an ordinal is skipped. The event object is sealed separately from the proposal and parser output; its counts derive solely by replay of transitions, not by array length or a second serialization of parser counters. The raw complete-result count E is established independently by a bounded top-level scanner before element evaluation; the scanner's exact bytes and count observation must be retained under its own versioned receipt (`scanner_observation_ref`) and compared with raw bytes. **Proposed attachment contract, not existing behavior:** at the keyed response-read handoff, the owner selects a single invocation-scoped event sink keyed by `(transaction_id,request_key,invocation_id,response_read_ref)` and attaches it to evaluator transitions before evaluation begins; the scanner runs against that same retained complete keyed raw response before the sink records the first element transition. Partial events after crash or sink failure are private, non-issuable and retained only under the bounded transaction policy. No parsed-item reconstruction or post-evaluation counter serialization qualifies as an independent event. Merely replaying a sink populated after parsing, or computing event records from `items`, does **not** satisfy independence. Required producer/sink attachment point, crash/partial-event behavior, scanner implementation identity and event/scanner schema digest are not established by this document and block acceptance; this proposal specifies required observable transitions, not proof that the implementation already records them.

### Retention and quarantine transaction

Assumption: proposal, candidate and final publication are separate commits, so a failure can leave verified private predecessors. Transaction ownership starts with the first successful owner-retained raw/method object and survives every incomplete publication; it transfers to the final's admitted dependency list **only after** final verified readback. Until then all intermediates are inactive, private, never root-selected as an issuing receipt, and accounted for in a transaction inventory with exact refs, byte lengths, state and transaction ID. On any precommit failure, record `PRECOMMIT_FAILED`, no invented committed ref, and retain already verified predecessors under the transaction policy. On committed verification failure at **any** stage, preserve the returned exact potentially committed selector and expected digest even if readback fails; append a redacted quarantine inventory entry `{transaction_id,stage,selector,expected_digest,byte_length,disposition:PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED}` under the same pinned root. At final committed verification failure, candidate/proposal remain inert, final potentially committed bytes are quarantined, and replay never upgrades them; new issuance requires a separately reviewed successor transaction and distinct final identity. Owner-only inventory access, no public selector enumeration; inventory has no raw payload, source body or provider error. No automatic retry or deletion. Reviewed retirement with zero active dependents, privacy revoke with cascading tombstones, failed deletion quarantine and unexplained absence follow the accepted lifecycle, including all transitive final dependencies. **Blocking decisions:** owner must select positive maximum transaction objects, aggregate bytes, per-inventory entries/bytes and elapsed retention, inventory key/selector custody, who can inspect quarantined metadata, expiration resolution for pre-final objects, and the final-failure/quarantine retention and privacy-revocation procedure. Neither the accepted 1,500,000-byte per-record cap nor the optional 24-hour framed diagnostics window supplies these aggregate bounds. Without selected bounds and a privacy policy digest, no issuance path may be enabled; this draft does not invent them.

`schemas/adr0011-references-issuance-records.proposed.schema.json` is a **draft-only Draft 2020-12 shape contract**, not a frozen implementation schema. It closes four role shapes, known field types and local outcome pairs; JSON Schema cannot compare dynamic counts with array lengths, ordinals with indices, repeated counts with each other, selector hashes with digest bytes, sort/uniqueness by role, event sequence transitions, or dependency completeness. Those must be checked by independent replay, including `known_e=observed_e_b=observed_e_t=whole_result_p`, candidate `len(occurrences)=proposed_p` with ordinals `0..P−1`, final `e=e_b=e_t=p=a=proposed_p`, and exact context/ref equality across records. A schema-valid document alone is **never** an issuance receipt. The source/revision/method/target/raw/scanner/policy predecessor schema identities remain unpinned and block implementation acceptance.

## Failure and diagnostic invariant

For **every publication or replay failure in this otherwise valid, fully evaluated success arm** before final verified readback—including method, raw result/event, proposal, candidate, or final precommit failure; committed-but-unverified bytes; missing or substituted replay dependencies—there is **no new issued request outcome, terminal or admitted occurrence**: issued `T=0,A=0`. Evaluation may already have observed `B=1`, `E=2,E_B=2,E_T=2,P=2` in the two-valid-element publication-failure controls, but those counts remain provisional and are not a newly issued ledger. Apply matrix rows 35–36 to failures at **each** success-arm publication stage, not only the first attempt. A committed verification failure must report `PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED`, retain the exact potentially committed selector/digest in a separately bounded, root-bound **redacted quarantine inventory**, and never retry, delete, or retroactively upgrade that receipt. A precommit failure reports `PRECOMMIT_FAILED` without inventing a committed selector. Failure inventories contain no raw result, source body, or unredacted provider error. The inventory's numeric bound, key/selector custody, owner access, retention and failed-final-readback behavior must be pinned before implementation acceptance.

This success-arm invariant does **not** erase mandatory diagnostic outcomes: independently replayed, bounded and redacted D receipts for keyed server errors or malformed complete results can issue `PROVIDER_FAILURE` or `MALFORMED` and their prescribed terminal counters without admitting occurrences, under the separately selected diagnostic policy. `WITHHELD` cannot silently become a verified diagnostic or an admission. The current failure path publishes an unredacted `ResultBase64` method object before its D record; it must be replaced or kept disabled, not treated as compliant by this amendment.

Persisted bytes that contain a *proposed* outcome or final intended counts but fail readback are inert: only the owner-returned, fully replayed composite receipt defines issued `T/A`. Quarantine is not an admission receipt. A later reviewed successor publication would have a distinct selector/digest and new transaction; it does not validate the original failure retroactively.

### Stage-wise status precedence and matrix rows 35–36

Assumption: only independently replayed receipts qualify as verified. At each stage `method/target prerequisite → raw/event → proposal → candidate → final`, classify transport/parse/policy failures under the accepted closed request-outcome order **only if their prescribed D or V evidence is independently issued**. Before success-arm final verification, `COMMITTED_VERIFICATION_FAILED` outranks any proposed `COMPLETE[_EMPTY]` and yields publication `PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED`, request outcome `—`, query disposition `NONE`, issued `T=A=0`. A precommit publication failure yields `PRECOMMIT_FAILED` with the same issuance zeros. Missing/substituted dependencies yield no new verdict and no new receipt, not a retroactive change to immutable historical issued bytes. A verified D diagnostic is a **different arm** and is not suppressed by this success-arm barrier; a withheld D remains `WITHHELD` rather than success or a manufactured diagnostic.

| Stage and fault | Row 35 precommit | Row 36 committed verification | Provisional observations / issued result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| method (and subordinate target prerequisite) | no new references V, N/`PRECOMMIT_FAILED` | quarantine exact committed method/target ref; N/`PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED` | if before evaluation B=0/E=U; otherwise preserve actually observed B/E/E_B/E_T/P; issued T=A=0 |
| bounded raw result or independent evaluator event | transaction predecessors only; N/`PRECOMMIT_FAILED` | quarantine exact raw/event ref; N/`PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED` | B and evaluation counts only if actually observed; issued T=A=0 |
| evaluation proposal | no candidate/final; N/`PRECOMMIT_FAILED` | quarantine proposal ref; N/`PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED` | full two-item control B=1,E/E_B/E_T/P=2; issued T=A=0 |
| inert candidate | no final; N/`PRECOMMIT_FAILED` | quarantine candidate ref; N/`PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED` | full two-item control B=1,E/E_B/E_T/P=2; issued T=A=0 |
| final issuance receipt | no final readback; N/`PRECOMMIT_FAILED` | quarantine **final** exact selector/expected digest; N/`PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED` | full two-item control B=1,E/E_B/E_T/P=2; issued T=A=0 |
| replay of any verified predecessor or final | N/`REPLAY_UNAVAILABLE_UNEXPLAINED` for unexplained absence, no new receipt | substituted/invalid bytes never verified; retain exact failed ref for investigation | no new verdict/T/A; original historical issued record, if any, is not rewritten |

For rows 35–36 at **every** publication stage after a fully observed valid two-item result, retain provisional `N=1,B=1,E=E_B=E_T=P=2,T=0,A=0`; stages before evaluation cannot fabricate B or E. The matrix's `PRECOMMIT_FAILED`/`PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED` are dispositions, not members of the closed request-outcome vocabulary. The publication interface must return a typed precommit failure with no committed selector or a typed committed-verification failure carrying the exact attempted selector and expected digest; an API that discards them cannot implement row 36. On simultaneous independent failures, fail closed and report the earliest causal stage, retaining **every** known potentially committed ref; never downgrade committed uncertainty to precommit failure. Independently replay and compare status, counters, outcome/disposition, each link and each original ordinal; no schema-only test establishes these relationships.

## Falsification requirements before independent references qualification

- For equal-location ordinals 0 and 1, and separately for exact `null`/`[]`, assert issued `T=A=0` before and after proposal/candidate readback; only successful final verification returns `T=1,A=2` (or `T=1,A=0` for empty). Check exact ordinal identity and compositional terminal binding. These are test-owned controls until real provider qualification.
- Independently force precommit failure and committed verification failure at method, proposal, candidate, and final publication/readback. Assert no issuing receipt, `T=A=0`, distinct provisional observed counts, exact selector/digest quarantine only for potentially committed bytes, and no retry/retro-upgrade. A corrupted final artifact must never admit an otherwise valid candidate.
- Substitute each proposal/candidate/final selector, digest, ordinal/range, policy/source/revision, count, status and cross-object dependency **one field at a time** with independent expected values. No substituted path issues a new terminal or occurrence. Preserve the original immutable historical bytes and accepted matrix substitution semantics.
- Test privacy withholding, retention dependencies, retirement and revocation tombstones separately. Exact framed-wire/result/Manager body caps and diagnostic redaction remain independent unresolved requirements; this amendment does not count their unexecuted rows as passes.

**Current review disposition: REVISE / STOP BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION ACCEPTANCE.** The stakeholder chose composite terminal binding in principle; this does not accept the delta. Exact nested role schemas, policy bytes/digests, event source/scanner attachment and bounded private transaction/quarantine lifecycle still require independent review; no role-name proposal above resolves those blockers. An eventual decision may be `ACCEPT_REFERENCES_ISSUANCE_BARRIER_DELTA_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION | REVISE | REJECT` only after the missing role schemas, policy bytes and event/retention contracts are pinned and the diagnostic arm is reconciled. Acceptance would permit implementation/testing of this narrow delta only, not production issuance, occurrence `QUALIFIED`, definition implementation, grouping or CLI/MCP exposure.
