# lsp-trace feature inventory

Use this workflow when bounded technical evidence must inform a feature inventory. The inventory is external to native lsp-trace graph, inspection, filter, community, instability, and capture-set artifacts.

Load references progressively:

- For evidence packet preparation, census inputs, accounting, provisional records, and reversible grouping, read [Preparation and grouping](references/preparation-and-grouping.md).
- For independent semantic review, stakeholder correction, acceptance, presentation, iteration, and stopping rules, read [Adjudication and acceptance](references/adjudication-and-acceptance.md).
- For structural command details or authority ceilings, also load the `lsp-trace` skill and its relevant reference.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Mechanical preparation cannot accept feature identity. Technical adjacency, CALLS overlap, communities, centrality, instability, repeated labels, capture-set co-membership, or matching names are structural evidence only. They may nominate or challenge a hypothesis; they cannot decide canonical identity, purpose, value, production use, merge/split outcome, coverage, confidence, or acceptance.

CALLS remains server-reported only. Capture sets preserve constituents without inferring cross-capture CALLS or acquiring native single-capture custody. Preserve bounded zero, `UNKNOWN`, failed/successful-empty states, and untouched ambiguity explicitly.

## Workflow

1. Retain a transport-neutral technical evidence packet and reconcile all seeds and counts.
2. Prepare external `PROVISIONAL` candidate records with exact typed evidence references, alternatives, rejection conditions, authority ceilings, and correction lineage.
3. Treat grouping as a reversible hypothesis. Communities and instability may organize review but do not change candidate identity or status.
4. Run independent semantic adjudication. Challenge unsupported merges/splits, borrowed union evidence, transitive-as-direct claims, and absence inferred from bounded zero or `UNKNOWN`.
5. Request stakeholder correction for product/domain meaning. Record corrections as revisions with predecessors and finite inventory-state deltas; never rewrite native evidence.
6. Accept an inventory only under an explicit external authority and acceptance contract. Otherwise stop as provisional, unresolved, externally adjudicated, accepted-boundary, or bounded-review-dry as applicable.

Do not invent future census or context command syntax. If discussing a proposed interface, label it `FUTURE/PROPOSED` and describe behavior rather than pretending the command exists.
