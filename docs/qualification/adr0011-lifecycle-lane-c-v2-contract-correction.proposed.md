# ADR 0011 Lane C v2 — lifecycle contract correction (PROPOSED ONLY)

**Review status: REVISE; no implementation authority.** This packet corrects the design of the accepted-for-implementation retention prerequisite and the subsequent proposed cutover/schema packet without replacing their bytes or treating the proposed V1 schema as accepted. The maintainer selected **Trusted local host**: one trusted installation owns the selected private root and an independently durable epoch/head anchor. Cross-process concurrency and crash/restart must fail closed. Hostile same-UID replacement or rollback of root, lock, anchor or records is outside the claim; hashes provide integrity checks against accidental change, not producer authentication or authority. No physical/backup erasure guarantee is made. Any authority choice not specified below must return for maintainer review, not be selected by self-hash.

## 1. Participant boundary and lock

Assumption: every conforming actor for this one trusted installation can reach the same pinned private-root identity and the same OS advisory lock. **Every entry point**, including raw/result/ledger and source publication, publication-selector creation, final-ledger admission, dependency ownership transitions, pin acquisition/release, derivative creation, active replay/new traversal/grouping/use, fence/child/root/terminal publication, reconciliation and cleanup/unlink, must acquire the *same exclusive root lock* before deciding eligibility or committing a mutation. Publication must stage preparation and arbitrary provider work **outside** the lock, then acquire it, re-read root/anchor/fence/ownership and validate its exact inputs before no-replace commit; if invalid, discard staging. A new-use reader holds the lock through its eligibility decision and acquisition of a durable use/pin lease; an unlocked long operation cannot rely on a formerly active snapshot. Release operations also serialize under the lock, and an unregistered writer/reader is outside the contract and must not be silently admitted. No lock is held across arbitrary provider calls or network waits.

The trusted host provisions the root and lock with a pinned canonical root handle/identity (device/inode and trusted installation identity), owner-only root and regular single-link 0600 lock file, no-follow descriptor-relative opens and descriptor identity checks before and after acquisition. The lock pathname must resolve to the provisioned inode; unlink/recreate, symlink/hard-link substitution, changed root identity or mismatched descriptor/path identity denies action even if a process holds a lock on an old inode. A bounded acquisition deadline, interrupted/failed lock operation, unsupported filesystem/platform lock semantics, missing trusted capability or unverifiable root/anchor is fail-closed; never downgrade to selector `sync.Mutex`. Automatic process-death release of the OS lock is not release of a durable fence. No claim of resistance to a hostile same-UID process changing local storage is implied.

## 2. Independently anchored epoch and restart

Assumption: the trusted installation, not a record claimant, chooses the canonical root identity, genesis head and first epoch in a provisioning act before any lifecycle record. Persist an independently selected durable head/epoch anchor **outside every claimant-writable fence descendant** (and not inferred from a directory scan, record self-hash or largest visible epoch); define its trust, access and atomic durable update protocol for independent review. Under lock, enumerate the complete bounded namespace of successors and references from the independently selected genesis/anchor to the current head, with explicit maximum objects, bytes, depth and work. Match exact identities, version, previous-head link, consecutive epoch, no-replace publication, readback and sync. Zero unexpected successors at the active boundary is required: a gap, competing successor/fork, duplicate decision, rollback below anchored head, unexpected future successor, ambiguous ordering, missing committed bytes or committed-but-unverified successor **blocks all admission, pins, new use and cleanup**. Do not skip a malformed successor to select a convenient older head. Anchor advancement must have an independently replayable prepare/commit and crash recovery contract: if record commit and anchor commit diverge or either is unverified, remain blocked until a separately reviewed reconciliation proves a unique continuation; never infer it from the claimants' hashes. Each lock acquisition replays the current durable head and fence; cached epoch is only a hint and stale cache cannot authorize use. An anchor that is missing, stale or rolled back relative to independently observed committed successors is not healed automatically.

A durable verified fence binds root, head/epoch predecessor, decision and closure snapshot and remains effective after lock release/crash. Partial fence commit, uncertain sync or readback blocks rather than restoring the previous eligible state. A verified abort can restore eligibility only with independent proof under lock that **no unlink was invoked**, that all original expected bytes still match, that no root cutover occurred and that the successor/head history is complete. If such proof is unavailable, uncertainty is terminal for automatic restoration; manual reviewed reconciliation may retain denial but cannot manufacture historical no-effect evidence.

## 3. Closure and one cutover

Assumption: original issuance and independently recorded live derivatives/pins are the ownership authorities, not filesystem proximity. Under the same lock independently enumerate a bounded, complete, transitive dependency and dependent/pin closure from immutable ledger refs and independently registered pins; freeze a sorted canonical snapshot binding root identity, fence/head epoch, decision, selected dependencies, complete dependent identities, pin identities, enumeration limits and an independently checkable completeness witness. Re-enumerate against the current anchored epoch before every unlink. Missing, cyclic, ambiguous, over-limit or newly admitted evidence blocks. A claimant-provided `dependent_set` alone does not prove completeness.

For **PRIVACY_REVOKE**, publish exactly one immutable tombstone child per member of that snapshot, including every transitive dependent, with unique dependent identity and exact snapshot/fence binding. Publish a root that names exactly that sorted set and every exact child identity/digest, no extras/duplicates, then verify no-replace result, directory sync, exact readback and complete child-set replay under lock. Only this verified root is the all-dependent eligibility cutover. Child-only or committed-unverified root is inert as a cutover while the fence continues to block new use; no unlink before verified root. For **RETIRE_DERIVATIVES_AND_REMOVE**, independently prove all dependent pins and active leases absent at fence selection and again immediately before **each** unlink; retirement cannot use a partial tombstone as a pin override. Revocation invalidates all dependents even with bytes present; retirement never deletes a pinned dependency. Both preserve historical issuance bytes and counts; logical invalidation is distinct from physical removal.

## 4. Irreversible unlink boundary and evidence roles

Before **each** unlink, while locked and after exact epoch, closure, pins, selector and expected byte/digest revalidation, publish and durably verify an immutable **pre-effect unlink intent** identifying decision, root, fence/anchored epoch, target selector's exact bytes/digest/length, unique attempt ordinal, observed pre-state and intended syscall target. A committed-but-unverified intent blocks action; publication/sync/readback failure means **do not invoke unlink**. Only then issue at most the identified unlink syscall. A separate immutable **post-effect observation** records attributed invocation/result (or unknown), post-readback, close outcome and parent-directory sync outcome, referencing the exact pre-effect intent. A further verified terminal may assert removal only when every target has an unbroken intent→invocation observation→absence/readback→sync chain and complete replay, with no missing/ambiguous attempt. Neither an intent nor filesystem absence proves syscall success.

If killed between the pre-effect intent and durable post-effect observation, the syscall may or may not have occurred: preserve `EFFECT_UNCERTAIN`, deny new use and further automatic removal. Absence now cannot prove this attempt succeeded; later reappearance of identical bytes cannot prove the attempt did not unlink. `ABORT_VERIFIED_NO_UNLINK` is available only on independently durable proof that invocation did not happen for **every** intent (including any prior attempts), together with matching original bytes and no cutover; a process report, missing log entry or current presence is not proof. An already missing target without an earlier verified terminal is `REPLAY_UNAVAILABLE_UNEXPLAINED`, not successful removal. An unlink-success report with failed or unknown directory sync is uncertain durability, never verified erasure. Retry needs a new reviewed decision/attempt and fresh observation, not a rewrite or reinterpretation of the old one. Once a verified root has cut over, reconciliation cannot restore active eligibility even if all bytes reappear; once any unlink may have occurred, no-no-effect abort is automatic.

## 5. Crash/replay table

| Crash boundary | Durable interpretation on restart under pinned lock/head | Permitted continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Before verified fence (including commit/readback/sync ambiguity) | No presumed safe old epoch if a successor might exist; committed-unverified blocks. | Reconcile unique head/absence independently; no pin/use/remove meanwhile. |
| After verified fence, before children | Fence denies affected use/pins; original bytes are held. | Replay closure/epoch; verified no-unlink abort only with independent proof, or resume reviewed operation. |
| Between child commits, before verified root | Children are inert; fence denies use and unlink. | Verify exact child set and head, complete root or retain block; never treat partial children as cutover. |
| After root commit but before verification/sync/readback | Root is committed-unverified, not cutover; fence still denies use; unlink prohibited. | Reconcile exact root and children under lock; no speculative upgrade. |
| After verified root, before unlink | All dependents inactive even though bytes remain. | Revalidate epoch/closure and begin individually evidenced unlink; never restore eligibility. |
| Before a verified pre-effect intent | No unlink authorized for that ordinal; earlier attempts retain their own states. | Fresh revalidation and new pre-effect intent only. |
| After verified pre-effect intent, before durable post-effect observation (including killed-before/after syscall) | Effect uncertain regardless of current absence/presence. | Keep fence and denial; no inferred success or no-effect abort absent independent invocation proof. |
| After post-effect observation, before verified parent-directory sync or terminal | Failed/unknown sync or terminal is uncertain; absence not durable success. | Reconcile without upgrading a partial attempt; terminal only from complete verified chains. |
| After verified terminal | Logical root cutover (if revoke) and verified removal classification persist; later byte reappearance cannot resurrect use. | Replay terminal and classify anomaly separately; no erasure/backup claim. |

Any missing byte without verified terminal evidence is unexplained loss. Reconciliation is monotone with respect to verified root and possible unlink: it may clarify an uncertainty from independently durable evidence, but cannot reverse an irreversible cutover or assert no unlink merely from present bytes. No stale in-memory state authorizes restart.

## 6. Required new schema contract, not selected wire authority

The current `adr0011-lifecycle-v1.proposed.schema.json` and its proposed evidence remain **inadequate and unreviewed** for this correction: V1 `unlinkAttempt` requires pre-state and syscall/post/sync outcomes in one record that cannot be durably committed before effect; its `previous_epoch_digest` and claimant-supplied sets do not independently establish head authority or closure. A new separately named/versioned schema, independently selected whole-file hash, role URIs, canonicalization, domain strings and selector policy require review **before implementation**; this packet does not invent an accepted URI, digest or pin. Required roles/fields include trusted-host root/genesis/anchor identity and durable head transition/recovery evidence; complete successor-enumeration bounds/status; fence-bound independent closure/pin snapshot with completeness witness; child/root exact membership and one-to-one linkage; distinct pre-effect intent (`root`, `epoch`, `fence`, `selector`, expected bytes/digest/length, `ordinal`, `pre_state`, durable-commit evidence); post-effect invocation/observation (`intent_ref`, syscall result/unknown, readback, close and parent-sync evidence); terminal exact attempt closure; and reconciliation with explicit independently proven no-invocation versus uncertain-effect outcomes. Shape validity never proves truth of host provisioning, pin completeness or syscall history. Reject duplicate selectors/ordinals, collisions, role confusion, forks and unbounded replay; absence of evidence cannot satisfy a positive proof obligation. The independent anchor placement/atomic update/recovery authority and independently durable no-invocation proof mechanism, if not already selected by the trusted-host provisioning decision, are **unresolved maintainer choices**, not self-hash defaults.

## 7. Bounded implementation falsification and stop boundary

Future implementation controls must use two independent processes on one provisioned root: contend for the same lock; race admission/pin/new-use against fence and every unlink; replace the lock pathname/inode while one process retains its descriptor; test timeout and unsupported platform; show no provider call under lock; test independently anchored stale cache, fork, gap, rollback, missing and committed-unverified head. Kill/restart at verified/unverified fence, each child, root commit/sync/readback, each pre-effect intent, immediately before and after syscall, post-effect observation, parent sync and terminal. Inject missing/reappearing bytes, incomplete closure, a newly registered pin, failed unlink/readback/sync and inability to prove no invocation. A second process must deny affected pin/use throughout uncertain states, with root alone causing all-dependent cutover; no fixture may infer removal from absence or no-effect from reappearance. Freeze exact root and object/byte/work limits and separately selected expected hashes before running fixtures; record original issuance bytes/counts unchanged before/after.

**Stop:** this packet authorizes only independent design review. Do not implement, mutate the V1 schema, execute cleanup or live/qualification rows, publish public references, or commit on its authority. Public references remain `T=A=0`, `0/162`; `PublishReferences` remains fail-closed. Source-pin manifests and historical bytes/counts remain immutable. Obtain explicit maintainer resolution of any remaining anchor/no-invocation authority choice, then review exact new schema and policy bytes and implementation evidence independently. The parent reviews this proposed packet; neither this text nor a hash grants acceptance.
