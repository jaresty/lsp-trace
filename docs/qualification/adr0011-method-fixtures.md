## Scope and evidence boundary

ADR 0011 is the controlling contract; the [private method-result grammar v1](adr0011-method-result-contract-v1.md) selects parser shapes for future tests only. In particular, query-target identity admission is specified at line 68; request aggregation and precedence at 110–122; exact occurrence multiplicity and cross-kind separation at 80–97; context selections, matching supplies and independent accounting at 136–150. Where this matrix does not spell out a result, use those clauses; do not infer a new method policy, normalization rule, numeric limit, or schema. A missing query-target receipt is a method-admission failure; a missing containing definition or body after valid method admission is a separate source-context disposition.

## Executed Darwin managed-process write control — not method admission

`sessionruntime/request_write_darwin_test.go` starts the existing `cmd/fake-lsp` under the local Darwin supervisor, waits for READY, sends one bounded `textDocument/definition` JSON-RPC request, and checks the child trace records that method once. The fake server does **not** advertise definition capability and returns its ordinary method-not-found code `-32601`. The test separately checks the manager's completed-write observation against the exact session/generation, generated request key, method, and independently reconstructed framed-request length/SHA-256, then stops and reaps its owned process. A controlled frame-digest perturbation failed `ASSERT_MANAGED_WRITE_PROCESS_EXACT_FRAME` before restoration. Offline affected-package tests, a focused runtime race run, vet, formatting, and diff checks passed; private logs are under `.pi/evidence/adr0011-managed-write-process/`.

This is manager-local completed-write evidence plus a test-owned process trace. It is **not** a provider-authenticated or immutable method receipt, proof of arbitrary provider delivery, verified capability advertisement, parsed definition result, terminal member denominator, query-target identity, source-context supply, or admitted occurrence. `-32601` is not mapped to ADR `UNSUPPORTED` by this test. None of D/R-01 through D/R-09 is promoted from proposed to qualified by this control; the remaining matrix expectations are still proposed.

## Executed test-owned managed method responses — selected D/R wire and parse

`internal/adr0011methodresult/method_wire_no_dispatch_darwin_test.go` exercises D-01/R-01 and D-08/R-08 with the same supervised test-owned peer. These private outcomes are **not** frozen ADR `UNSUPPORTED` or a provider-authenticated capability receipt.

## Qualification and unresolved contract

These fixtures are candidate method/source-context inputs, not the full ADR 0011 qualification. Private grammar v1 selects `null` and `LocationLink` parsing; the `null`/`[]` managed subset and `LocationLink` in-process subset have been exercised, but no parser or admission implementation has been accepted. Before qualification, freeze the complete method request/receipt and admission contracts, capability receipt representation, member outcome vocabulary, range/encoding and custody representation, bound values, and source-context disposition schema. Do not infer endpoint normalization, deduplication, preferred targets, or threshold values from the fixtures.

A passing fixture suite alone does not qualify a grouping policy, authorize Leiden, enable CLI/MCP/public exposure, or accept feature identity. ADR 0011 requires the separately frozen corpus/held-out evaluation, policy and threshold qualification, deterministic replay/accounting, and independent maintainer decision; public enablement and any later consumer policy remain separate gates. Until applicable gates pass, fail closed and retain the ADR claim ceiling.
