# ADR 0011 — references issuance-barrier decision handoff

**Decision requested from the receiving maintainer:** `ACCEPT_REFERENCES_ISSUANCE_BARRIER_DELTA_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION | REVISE | REJECT`. The current independent decision is **REVISE**. This packet is a request to close the *implementation contract*, not permission to issue production references or a request for a nontechnical stakeholder to choose byte counts.

## Why this is needed

The current test-owned references chain can publish a terminal claiming `T=1,A=P` before the occurrence ledger is verified. If a later publication/readback fails, persisted bytes appear to claim admission even though the accepted matrix requires **no newly issued terminal or occurrence (`T=A=0`)**. Its malformed-result diagnostic path also publishes a method object containing raw result bytes before it publishes a redacted diagnostic. Neither path is production-enabled.

The proposed correction is an acyclic **evaluation proposal → inert occurrence candidate → final issuance receipt**. Only successful publication, byte-exact readback, and independent replay of the *final* receipt may issue `T=1,A=P`; failures leave `T=A=0`. A separate owner-only raw artifact permits replay of malformed results without putting those bytes in the redacted diagnostic `D`. These two ideas were selected **in principle**, not accepted as an implementation contract. The accepted references contract and matrix at `ed91b585` remain unchanged; historical omitted-selector `CALLS_ONLY` must remain byte-compatible.

## What the controls protect (not a questionnaire for stakeholders)

| Control | What goes wrong without it | Who must propose and substantiate it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact predecessor versions, selectors, and digests | A well-shaped ref can name the wrong method/target or a self-asserted source/policy. A final receipt could appear verified while its dependencies are not. | References contract owner: pin actual predecessor record grammars and owner-computed identities; reviewer tests substitutions. |
| Independent scanner and evaluator-transition record | Parser item counts or an after-the-fact copy of `items` can manufacture `E`, `E_B/E_T`, or `P` and thereby `A`. Transport write/read observations do **not** prove evaluator transitions. | References implementation owner: specify the response-read→scanner→transition-sink seam, keyed invocation, failure behavior, receipt schemas/identities, and independent replay. Do not claim the current `evaluateCompleteReferences` event slice is independent. |
| Result, frame, Manager aggregate, message, work, allocation, and deadline ceilings | A 1 MiB raw result can be rejected earlier by Manager's aggregate decoded-message-body cap; raising that cap alone currently disables owned-pair capture. A large frame or malformed payload could consume resources or escape redaction. These measure **different bytes**, so one cap cannot stand for another. | Runtime/transport owner: measure the exact boundaries, select justified positive limits, keep owned capture eligible, and supply boundary and over-limit tests. The proposed 4 MiB Manager/wire limits are *candidates*, not authority. |
| Private raw access and redacted `D` | Malformed result bytes may contain secrets; publishing `ResultBase64`, logs, errors, or public selectors defeats later redaction. Conversely, a redacted summary alone cannot replay arbitrary malformed JSON. | Privacy/security owner: authorize a named owner-only raw custodian and a redacted-metadata reviewer policy, or prohibit raw replay and leave corresponding matrix rows `INCOMPLETE`/`WITHHELD`. Verify canary absence across all public/error/log/quarantine sinks. |
| Bounded transaction inventory, quarantine, and retention | A committed-but-unverified final can remain on disk; deleting it silently or treating it as issued is unsafe. Unlimited failed transactions can exhaust disk. Auto-expiry could delete a still-needed replay dependency. | Publication/retention owner with privacy owner: propose object/aggregate/entry/byte caps, access roster, elapsed review ceiling, backup and retirement rules. On cap or deadline breach **block new issuance and seek reviewed resolution**; do not auto-promote, retry, or delete active dependencies. |

The draft private-raw profile proposes raw ≤1,048,576 bytes/object, ≤16 objects/16 MiB per acquisition and a transaction envelope of 256 objects/128 MiB, plus 256 redacted quarantine entries/512 KiB. These are unapproved **candidate** numbers. The user declined to select among numeric/access/retention options; **do not interpret that as approval, rejection of the feature, or authority to choose on their behalf**. Technical owners should return a justified bounded proposal (or state why no safe profile exists), which the independent maintainer can accept or revise.
