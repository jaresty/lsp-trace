# Evidence boundaries

## Authority classes

- Caller-supplied: provenance, requested limits/configuration, server command/arguments, explicit environment names, and original labels/positions.
- Tool-derived: normalized identities, digests, memberships, counters, boundaries, and commitments derived from recorded inputs/observations.
- Server-reported: capabilities, prepared targets, Call Hierarchy relations, Type Hierarchy associations, document-symbol candidates, diagnostics, and opaque server data.
- Custody: only the exact integrity/custody commitment documented by the receipt.

None authenticates hidden inputs or producer identity unless an external contract explicitly does so.

## Relation separation

CALLS records are server-reported Call Hierarchy relations only. Do not manufacture CALLS from text, relation adapters, dispatch families, callbacks, state flow, rendering, tasks, configuration, communities, or capture proximity.

Dispatch relationships and sibling candidates are discovery nominations with zero CALLS support. Communities, PageRank, hubs, component structure, and instability are structural analysis only. Instability says how a bounded structural result changes under declared perturbation; it is not confidence, truth, feature cohesion, or acceptance.

Keep evidence namespaces separate. A matching raw identifier across namespaces is not an evidence join. Transitive paths are not direct support. Shared global records referenced by multiple seeds are not independent observations.

## Completeness and ambiguity

`traversal_complete` means the requested server-relative bounded walk completed. `source_graph_complete` remains `UNKNOWN`. Exit success, an empty response, bounded zero, no selected symbols, or zero group assignments never proves source, endpoint, runtime, callable, or feature absence.

Preserve:

- failed, null, timed-out, canceled, and budget-truncated responses;
- successful-empty responses;
- active node/depth/request/time bounds;
- unsupported capabilities and omitted dynamic/generated/reflective/configuration/template/framework relationships;
- `UNKNOWN` provenance or completeness;
- ambiguity that no admitted evidence changed.

Do not silently resolve or discard untouched ambiguity. A later round may narrow, split, or retain it; otherwise report it unchanged.

## Attribution

Every requested seed has one corresponding result, including failure. Attribute only exact per-seed memberships and references. The union graph is a deduplicated carrier, not every seed's evidence. Diagnostic correlation is not custody or causation.

Native successful slice membership is the bounded outgoing closure plus incoming closure from only that seed's reached upward starts. Discovery nominations are never borrowed as CALLS.

## Capture sets and custody

Capture sets preserve exact constituents and their identities. They may expose a deterministic union of constituent native records but do not infer cross-capture CALLS, merge provenance into one observation, or gain native single-capture custody. Recomposition success and structural compatibility do not authorize native-schema promotion.

Exact-byte digests identify bytes; semantic digests commit to documented canonical content; generation names are publication coordinates. None independently proves authenticity, source truth, runtime behavior, or feature meaning.

## Consumer ceiling

Structural evidence can support investigation of code relationships. It cannot by itself establish proposition admission, canonical feature identity/name, purpose, user value, production use, priority, lifecycle, confidence, complete coverage, merge/split acceptance, or stakeholder acceptance. Mechanical preparation must stop before those decisions.
