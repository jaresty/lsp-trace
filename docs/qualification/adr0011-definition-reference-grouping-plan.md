# ADR 0011 definition/reference grouping — qualification-plan draft

**State:** UNFROZEN DRAFT. Not a `GroupingPolicyQualificationReceipt`, a grouping policy, an implementation acceptance, or permission to run Leiden on definition/reference input. [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-versioned-definition-reference-grouping-evidence.md) now requires grouping **with CALLS, without CALLS, and with CALLS plus admitted definition/reference evidence**. References-only, definitions-only, and references-plus-definitions are eligible explicit no-CALLS inputs; historical omitted selection remains `CALLS_ONLY`. A held-out zero-CALLS CUE arm is one required proof of the general no-CALLS capability, not its exclusive product scope. The exact `CUE_<PROVIDER>_EXACT_V1` provider, corpus, and policy are not yet selected. `GO_GOPLS_EXACT_V1` and the accepted references-occurrence implementation contract do not qualify CUE or admit definition occurrences; the new family remains disabled until its separate gates pass.

Exact framed JSON-RPC request/response capture and its 24-hour replay window are optional private diagnostics, **not** a gate for D/R occurrence admission or Leiden. The required immutable method record instead binds a canonical validated query, keyed managed transaction and exact bounded result payload (or explicit ineligible withholding), plus a separately verified terminal-member ledger and reference query-target identity. **User-selected first references target derivation:** unique server-reported `textDocument/documentSymbol` selection-range containment at the exact predeclared query point, under its own admitted method/source/version custody; an ambiguous or missing selection yields no reference target. A caller-built source-context point/span is not that receipt. **User-selected canonical payload retention direction:** private, default-off, bounded immutable query/result payload through an explicit qualification-review lifecycle, followed by reviewed removal; no automatic 24-hour expiry inherited from optional frame diagnostics. Ownership, limits, deletion accounting, and exact retention policy remain to be frozen before production publication. Like CALLS, this is not producer-authenticated. Historical CALLS admission and bytes are unchanged.

## Required comparison and delivery arms

Run each applicable arm on its pinned corpus partition, keeping evidence-family identity and occurrence custody visible. Historical CALLS-only behavior remains unchanged; no-CALLS and mixed CALLS-plus-D/R grouping are **required qualified delivery results**, not mere adversarial controls. Compare arms on the same exact inputs where the provider and corpus support both; never fabricate absent CALLS to force parity:

| Arm | Admitted input | Claim ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical baseline | CALLS-only, omitted selector | Preserve historical bytes, outputs, replay, and CALLS-specific representative/outward-consumer semantics. |
| References | `REFERENCES_SYMBOL` occurrences only | Query-target receipt required; references cannot establish `CALLS` or choose representatives. |
| Definitions | `RESOLVES_TO_DEFINITION` occurrences only | One occurrence per returned target; no first-target selection or runtime claim; its admission contract remains separate and unstarted. |
| **No-CALLS delivery** | Explicit admitted references-only, definitions-only, or references-plus-definitions; **zero CALLS**, including pinned `CUE_<PROVIDER>_EXACT_V1` held-out D/R-only proof | Useful qualified membership with representative unavailable and terminal `NO_CALLS_REPRESENTATIVE` for every community; no runtime, caller, outward-consumer or ownership claim. |
| **Mixed delivery** | Named eligible CALLS plus one or both admitted definition/reference layers under an explicit versioned policy | Preserve every original occurrence, policy action, exclusion, and derived `PairWeight`; CALLS-only representative/outward-consumer rules inspect eligible CALLS only and never relabel D/R as CALLS. |

An unsupported method, ambiguous query-target receipt, partial result, or absent qualified policy is a typed outcome—not an empty graph and not permission to substitute another arm. A no-CALLS community has `NO_CALLS_REPRESENTATIVE`.

## Test and evidence matrix

- **Acquisition and custody:** capability-aware bounded `textDocument/references` and `textDocument/definition`; exact request/method/query-target receipts; separate `QUERY_OCCURRENCE`, `REFERENCING_OCCURRENCE`/`REFERENCING_SYMBOL`, `REFERENCED_SYMBOL`, `DEFINITION_TARGET` roles. Preserve provider, session generation, workspace revision/custody, URI, range, encoding, document version, and digest. Test `UNSUPPORTED`, `COMPLETE_EMPTY`, `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, `MALFORMED`, timeout, cancellation, provider failure, resource/policy/revision failures and balanced member denominators.
- **Source-bearing context:** test both methods with exact occurrence ranges, independently resolved unique containing/query and target full-definition display ranges, bounded exact document supplies and returned bodies. Test cross-document context; ambiguous containment, identifier-only server item ranges, missing/version-changed documents, privacy withholding, independent document/range/byte/work limits, and per-candidate terminal accounting. A valid method occurrence must survive projection failure, while context-complete Describe must not be claimed without both required bodies. Source ranges must never manufacture a relation or repair a query-target receipt.
- **Source-efficient sharing:** for one exact source point used by a call, reference, and definition, emit one source-point selection and, when full display identity matches, one body-bearing context span with three typed occurrence bindings. Different exact evidence ranges may share a span but not a point. Vary one of session generation, revision, document version, URI, source digest, encoding, display range, display receipt, projection/privacy policy or disposition at a time: forbid ineligible reuse and observe terminal accounting. Permuting occurrences must preserve IDs, emitted bytes, and all three relation denominators; forged or absent span IDs fail closed. Test shared spans across two source points without merging evidence.
- **Shared MVP traversal:** exercise one selected typed walk over an admitted CALLS → definition → reference chain and its reverse, with `up_depth`/`down_depth` independently set to 0, 1, and 2. Assert canonical direction, hop/frontier and node-bound accounting across kinds, exact per-kind occurrence counts and omissions, permutation stability, cycles and repeated endpoints without cross-kind deduplication. Incoming definition traversal uses admitted reverse-index evidence only; no inverse definition method, inferred reference target, implicit LSP fallback, or claim of completeness. An omitted selector remains byte-identical CALLS-only. This is a prerequisite for traversal qualification, not Leiden admission or public enablement.
- **Admission and composition:** exact occurrence multiplicity and canonical order, non-CALLS relation orientation, complete receipt and ledger bindings, closed family/version/kind unions, duplicate keys, trailing data, digest and custody substitution rejection. Use one symbol with simultaneous `CALLS`, `REFERENCES_SYMBOL`, and `RESOLVES_TO_DEFINITION` occurrences: all three typed ledgers and exact witnesses survive; shared endpoints or display spans cannot deduplicate across kinds. Account for every excluded, capped, normalized, or downweighted occurrence without rewriting the ledger.
- **Required no-CALLS and mixed held-out modes:** preregister separate expected groupings and metrics for eligible no-CALLS and mixed CALLS-plus-D/R inputs on applicable profiles. Zero CALLS is valid input, not `COMPLETE_EMPTY`; a mixed result cannot omit a declared relation family or infer CALLS from D/R. For communities with no eligible CALLS, membership can be available but the representative is unavailable and terminal status is `NO_CALLS_REPRESENTATIVE`.
- **Required held-out CUE zero-CALLS corpus (one no-CALLS proof):** preregister useful expected communities from references and definitions alone; measure false merges and fragmentation of distinct CUE packages under builtin, external, import/re-export and definition/reference hubs. Frozen policy must predictably exclude or downweight unresolved external and builtin references, preserve unmatched members, reproduce canonical groupings under input permutation, and issue `NO_CALLS_REPRESENTATIVE` with membership available and representative unavailable for **every** community. Compare labels without runtime, caller, consumer, or ownership inference.
- **Adversarial corpora:** builtin/standard-library and import/re-export hubs; generated registries; shared helpers; repeated provider returns; aliases and multi-target definitions; unresolved external and cross-language/revision cases; reference-only communities with no CALLS representative. Compare false merges and fragmentation against preregistered labels.
- **Reproducibility and compatibility:** identical canonical bytes under exact replay environment and input permutation; changed family, receipt, ledger, policy, numeric contract, seed, limit, or partition changes identity. Historical CALLS-only selectors, schemas, snapshots, packets, checkpoints, CLI/MCP behavior, and output bytes stay unchanged. CUE and Go qualification must agree on canonical contracts and terminal outcomes.
- **Privacy and bounds:** declared messages, requests, bytes, documents, candidates, occurrences, time and work; terminal accounting at each limit; no network/ambient-workspace fallback during replay and no source leakage in public failures.

## Staged shared-depth and candidate-walk observations (not traversal qualification)

A private relation-neutral `internal/directedwalk.Depths` computes bounded forward and reverse endpoint depths. Existing CALLS projection delegates its depth calculation to that helper; `TestCallsDirectedDepthsRemainStableAcrossSharedKernel` passed before and after the adapter change. A synthetic mixed-kind chain `CALLS → RESOLVES_TO_DEFINITION → REFERENCES_SYMBOL` exercises 0/2/3-hop up/down depths, a cycle, and repeated endpoint pairs without rewriting typed fixture occurrences.

Private `internal/directedwalk.Walk` now projects **caller-supplied, unverified candidates** over explicitly selected kinds with one deterministic output-node budget. It retains candidate ID/kind/orientation, parallel and cross-kind occurrences, directional witnesses, node-bound and out-of-frontier dispositions, and per-kind counts. An adversarial test proved that using full-graph up depths incorrectly creates an upstream path through an omitted node; recomputing depths on the retained induced subgraph passed after that perturbation was removed. A separately declared `MaxCandidates` (private ceiling 10,000) counts **all** supplied candidates, even unselected kinds, before candidate-field validation or graph allocation; an over-limit request returns a typed error and no partial result, while the exact limit retains the previous projection. A separate `MaxWork` (private ceiling 1,000,000) now meters the whole private walk's defined **logical graph operations**: selector/candidate iterations, deterministic merge decision/write/copy slots, four depth traversals' edge/adjacency/queue iterations, node selection, and occurrence/count iterations. It returns an empty result and typed work-limit error on exhaustion, and successful `WorkUsed` is replay-stable and accepts its exact limit. A fixed-phase guard caught a deliberately unmetered incoming depth pass (166→150 units). These provisional numbers are not a frozen public policy. Logical units and candidate cardinality do **not** bound string comparison bytes, CPU time, memory allocation, caller input construction, or live method acquisition; qualification still needs those independent limits. Candidate IDs are not method/target/occurrence receipts, and `NODE_BOUND` is local graph-projection accounting, not a qualified request/member outcome. No caller may route unverified method results into a claimed admitted traversal.

A private `programcadmission.PrepareTypedInput` staging seam now projects only already-admitted composite CALLS occurrences with explicit `CALLS` kind, distinct occurrence identities, canonical direction, original relation-local call-site ordinals, multiplicity, and the unchanged composite source claim ceiling. The ordinal is carried only inside the opaque computation admission/staging path, not added to the historical serialized CALLS artifact. It rejects missing, duplicate, unknown, definition, or reference selections and rejects every caller-supplied method candidate before projecting anything. It does **not** feed Leiden, compose D/R, issue a method receipt, or qualify a D/R grouping policy; its `v0` identifier is explicitly not the ADR 0011 input-family version. The historical omitted-selector path is not routed through it. A synthetic same-ID/different-content `graph.Node` pair must still fail at the existing composite `NODE_CONFLICT` gate before typed staging; the private test checks that branch. The admitted mixed traversal, bounded acquisition and work, verified reverse index, terminal member denominators, public selector/byte compatibility qualification, and grouping-policy qualification remain open. These private tests neither make the selected ADR architecture operational nor establish source completeness, custody, or feature identity.

## Closed decision gate

The immutable `GroupingPolicyQualificationReceipt` binds the exact policy digest, evaluation-plan and corpus-manifest identities, fixture and held-out partitions, implementation/environment identities, metric definitions and complete results, report digest, independent reviewer identities, decision timestamp, and predecessor lengths/digests. Its only decisions are `QUALIFIED`, `REJECTED`, and `INCOMPLETE` under ADR 0011's rules. Missing preregistration, incomplete denominator, absent independent acceptance, incompatible identity, or unavailable CUE/Go parity yields `INCOMPLETE`; failed preregistered criteria or declined acceptance yields `REJECTED`. Only a strictly verified `QUALIFIED` receipt with matching policy digest may admit Leiden; this draft supplies none.
