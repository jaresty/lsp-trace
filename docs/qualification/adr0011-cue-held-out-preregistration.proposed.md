## Selection and freeze order

1. Enumerate actual managed CUE provider candidates from host configuration. Record binary path/version/SHA-256, launch arguments, source provenance, negotiated encoding, advertised `textDocument/references` and `textDocument/definition` capabilities, and bounded reproducible probes of **both** methods. An unavailable or unverified provider means `INCOMPLETE`; do not substitute document symbols for either method.
2. Nominate an accessible CUE repository with license/access permission, full clean Git commit, remote, submodules/dependencies, content manifest, and reproducible setup. Freeze the eligible provider/repository/revision tuple **before** revealing held-out labels. Historical session names or local source text do not establish that tuple. Real CUE occurrence acquisition also requires a separately qualified exact provider profile; Go/gopls receipts cannot be relabeled CUE.
3. Publish a deterministic selection rule over eligible packages and versions; partition calibration and held-out packages before labeling. Freeze exact package/file digests, exclusion rules, declared query URIs/ranges, method/capability/source/revision custody, bounds, and a capture plan. Candidate adversarial motifs to find, not assertions that they exist: unrelated packages sharing a builtin/import, common generated binding, local definition with multiple references, alias/multi-target definition, shared helper, unresolved external target, and at least one zero-CALLS corpus with two independently distinguishable communities.
4. Have two CUE-literate reviewers blind to graph partitions and each other's labels independently annotate source-grounded memberships and must-separate package pairs, citing exact ranges and marking ambiguous/unmatched items. A third independent adjudicator records disagreements and rationale. Freeze adjudicated label bytes under the custody protocol below **before** held-out execution; implementers must not inspect them while freezing edge projection or policy.

## Prespecified arms and measurements (proposed, not accepted)

- Arms: omitted-selector historical `CALLS_ONLY` byte/schema compatibility; explicit references-only; definitions-only; references-plus-definitions; and mixed CALLS-plus-D/R on corpora where CALLS evidence is admitted. **Selected for this proposed zero-CALLS arm:** `EXPLICIT_NO_CALLS_V1` sets **zero eligible CALLS contributions by policy**, with the exact reason `CALLS_EXCLUDED_BY_ARM`; retain separately whether CALLS was not acquired, unsupported, acquired-empty, unadmitted or present-but-excluded, along with supplied/excluded counts. None of those states is interchangeable with another or evidence that the provider has no calls. A hidden CALLS contribution rejects the arm. Membership may be available while the representative is unavailable; `NO_CALLS_REPRESENTATIVE` is a terminal status, **not** a representative identity.
- Denominators: source-grounded evaluable labeled members and same-label / must-separate pairs, plus separately enumerated excluded, ambiguous and unavailable cases. Measure false merges (must-separate pairs co-clustered), fragmentation (same-label pairs split), exact replay/permutation stability of canonical partition bytes/IDs, designated hub collapse (unrelated groups joined through builtin/import/generated/shared-helper hubs), unmatched/no-community rate, per-arm coverage and resource use. Report zero-denominator arms as `INCOMPLETE`, not perfect scores.
- Candidate preregistration criteria for independent review **before** execution: zero false merges on enumerated must-separate pairs; at least 80% same-label evaluable pairs retained together; 100% byte-identical partitions on exact replay and prescribed input permutations; zero hub-mediated joins of designated unrelated groups. These numbers are proposals, not chosen or tuned thresholds. Record confidence intervals and sensitivity against the calibration corpus; never revise held-out criteria using held-out labels.

## Held-out label custody protocol (proposed, not executed)

A named corpus custodian publishes a deterministic package split and immutable source manifest; two independent CUE labelers submit separately sealed exact-range label artifacts without seeing each other's work or graph partitions. A third adjudicator alone reconciles disagreements, records excluded/ambiguous items and immutable final label bytes. The custodian stores each versioned artifact under a private 0700 root with no-replace readback, exact digest, independent access log and a blinded pre-reveal commitment (salt secret until reveal so small label sets cannot be guessed from a public digest). Only the custodian, adjudicator as needed, and designated evaluation actor may read labels; policy implementers and threshold selectors have **no** read access. No person may serve as both labeler/adjudicator and policy tuner without independent review of the conflict. Name actors and freeze access controls before actual corpus capture; this draft names roles, not people.

The custodian records a one-way **policy freeze event** with exact grouping-input/endpoints, schema/policy/weights/thresholds, implementation and corpus digests plus reviewer sign-off. Only after that event may the evaluator obtain the sealed labels, run a bounded preregistered one-shot evaluation and publish a replayable metric record with denominators and exclusions; no raw held-out labels or iterative feedback go to implementers before the independent decision. Record reveal timestamp, actor, exact artifact digests, attempted accesses and any breach; access violation, post-reveal policy change, repeated tuning or missing audit yields `INCOMPLETE` and a newly sequestered corpus, not a patched held-out score. No actual access control or reveal is claimed here.

## Schema and custody boundaries to draft independently

`ReferenceGroupingInput` needs a closed additive versioned selector and exact immutable admitted occurrence-ledger references (kind, direction, original ordinal/multiplicity, method/target/terminal custody, provider/revision, query/result ranges, limits, predecessor digests). Mapping occurrence endpoints to stable grouping nodes requires the separate proposed [grouping endpoint identity policy](adr0011-grouping-endpoint-identity.proposed.md); transaction-scoped target candidates are **not** stable nodes. A distinct grouping policy record needs versioned eligible relation kinds and projection, hub-action vocabulary, contribution accounting, weights and numeric bounds, seed/resource limits, corpus/preregistration digests, metric definitions and independent qualification-receipt selector. Unknown/substituted fields reject; omission preserves historical `CALLS_ONLY` bytes. This document authorizes **neither schema implementation nor PairWeight/Leiden changes**.

**Reuse boundary:** immutable publication, canonical envelopes, receipt linking, retention/tombstone and replay mechanisms may be provider-neutral **only** if their own schemas/policies are versioned and proven compatible. CUE-specific provider binary/capabilities, language range/target resolver, exact method semantics, source/encoding, byte/work limits, Git revision, acquisition/admission policy IDs and real-fixture qualification must be distinct; Go/gopls receipts cannot be relabeled. The language-neutral occurrence orientation and ordinal rule does not itself qualify CUE parsing, source custody or grouping-node resolution.

**Open decisions:** real provider executable and repository/revision; eligible package split; named labelers/adjudicator/custodian/evaluator/maintainer; proposed criteria and denominators; exact capture privacy; CUE-specific occurrence qualification path. A held-out CUE proof requires independently admitted references **and** definition occurrences plus grouping-input and policy qualification. Do not infer that design preparation crosses those gates.
