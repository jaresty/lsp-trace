## Frozen proposed boundary

1. **Before invocation.** The concrete managed host selects a private 0700 root, an exact READY Go/gopls session/generation, clean registered Git worktree and pinned commit, prepared source/version/encoding, a unique document-symbol query target, the four original policy payloads, selected schema/implementation IDs, collision-safe invocation, exact managed request key, and a bounded plan. It retains a declaration, witness policy, initiation and separately rooted host-custody original under pre-invocation selections. Compare the custody original and its immutable expected ref held **outside** claimant manifest/ledgers; an in-record `PRE_INVOCATION` flag is not timing evidence. If target identity is unresolved, use an incomplete inventory without fabricated target/result, without final, and without invoking references.
2. **At the managed boundary.** Retain independently selected ordered WRITE and READ originals, exact key/session/generation/wire ID, strict `textDocument/references` JSON-RPC 2.0 request and success response, exact nested params grammar, matched raw result bytes and source. A keyed begin requires an actual matching observation. The historical owner-read, response-read, raw-result, scanner, events, proposal and candidate remain under their original schema IDs; do not rewrite them to claim no-result or UNKNOWN states they cannot represent. Errors, missing responses and `COMMITTED_VERIFICATION_FAILED` remain nonissuable, not empty success.
3. **After observation.** A host-selected immutable attempt-evidence manifest selects initiation and custody plus PRESENT or ABSENT_NOT_OBSERVED begin, terminal journal and scanner originals. Terminal journal independently witnesses a real method outcome; scanner E is an integer only for a complete bounded top-level count, otherwise `UNKNOWN`. Absence is not zero, and no unbegun or unterminated attempt receives a fabricated terminal. Complete-item accounting derives N/B/T/E/E_B/E_T/P/A from original observations and the parsed/admitted subset. Failed and unknown attempts may retain terminal/accounting evidence without a final; verified COMPLETE_EMPTY may have P=A=0, while COMPLETE/ITEMS with P>0/A=0 has no final.
4. **Ancillary closure.** The production inventory selects exactly three separate original-byte ancillary refs: terminal ledger, admitted-occurrence ledger (including a genuine empty set), and canonical accounting record. It also selects the attempt manifest as a **distinct** validation witness, not a fourth ancillary ledger. Ledgers depend only on selected historical and attempt originals, never on the inventory/final. Accounting traverses both ledgers, verifies the admitted unique identity subset and method precedence, and binds exact target/result, declaration/request/invocation and policy IDs. `final.context.accounting_digest = SHA-256(complete canonical accounting-record bytes)`, never candidate/proposal/invocation/ledger/final digest as surrogate.
5. **Final and publication.** The final retains **exactly 21 immediate `dependency_refs`**, one of each accepted role, byte-sorted under the accepted ref rule; neither inventory, manifest nor ancillary refs are appended. Replay complete transitive originals and expected host selections before no-replace final publication. Only independent no-replace publication evidence, exact post-read verification, and a fresh independently expected final readback can issue a typed receipt. An embedded `verification` claim or a test boolean is not proof. Any stale generation, unknown role, missing original, competing selector, changed byte, incomplete journal, wrong accounting, failed/unverified publication or fresh readback failure yields no new T/A and no public success.

## Proposed implementation ordering and review boundary

No implementation may select these new proposed role URIs as **accepted production identity** before this freeze and the complete schema/role bytes receive independent contract-only acceptance. After acceptance, the first writer may add **private/default-off** host-owned custody/publication machinery with synthetic guards, preserving `internal/adr0011methodresult.PublishReferences` fail-closed and historical `CALLS_ONLY` behavior. Keep the production final and ancillary closure out of public/CLI/MCP surfaces. Prove exact original-byte publication, ordering, no-replace and independently verified readback with focused fault-injection tests; an in-memory map or successful schema validation is insufficient. A separate frozen production technical-checkpoint packet and independent terminal review precede any request for live authorization.

Remaining coherent-substitution work is **unexecuted**, even where a synthetic guard exists: transaction/query/declaration and nonce, session/generation/wire ID, target/result/source, occurrence ordinal/URI/range/identity, policy/schema/implementation pins, manifest/inventory/ancillary selectors, and accounting. Each attack must rehash descendants and reach its intended semantic guard after earlier closure; an unchanged expected host original may reject earlier, but must be described as such. A different genuinely observed returned Location is not by itself an attack. If complete rehash of owner-held expectations removes the independent comparator, stop at an authority decision rather than invent an oracle.

**Independent review request:** verify every pinned input and the exact 21-ref/three-ancillary/manifest split; adjudicate original-byte selection and externally held pre-invocation custody, completed/failed/unknown state semantics, method-versus-publication ordering, and final readback without self-attestation. Return `ACCEPT_CONTRACT_FOR_PRIVATE_DEFAULT_OFF_IMPLEMENTATION_ONLY` or `REVISE` with concrete counterexample and first violated clause. This acceptance would permit private implementation work, not production issuance, a live row, or Lane B lifecycle acceptance. Lane B tombstones/cutover/crash/lease/revocation and Lane C private usability planning have separate decisions.
