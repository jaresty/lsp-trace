## Corrected staged-key requirement

1. **Before WRITE:** The concrete host freezes and independently retains the exact declaration/plan, chosen source and policy/schema/implementation pins, READY session/generation, collision-safe invocation, witness policy and `attemptHostCustody` plus N=1 `attemptInitiation`. Custody and initiation contain **no actual request key** and reject one as an extra property. An unresolved target has no WRITE, no key observation, B=T=0 and E=UNKNOWN: retain an incomplete inventory with absent target/result rather than a dummy key or completed 21-ref closure. A flag claiming `PRE_INVOCATION` does not establish timing; an independently held expected custody ref and original-byte readback must do so.
2. **At/after an actual completed framed WRITE:** The owner selects an independently retained `attemptRequestKeyObservation` original that binds the actual manager-assigned request key, wire ID and exact written-frame digest/length/selector to declaration, prewrite custody ref, invocation, session/generation and witness policy. A host-held expected ref and manager-owned completed-WRITE observation are outside claimant manifest/ledgers. The observation alone does **not** prove a matching READ, provider receipt or method success. A WRITE without independently verified bytes/key may only be incomplete and nonissuable.
3. **After response:** `attemptEvidenceManifest.key_observation` is PRESENT with the role-specific ref, or ABSENT_NOT_OBSERVED without ref or key. No key observation implies no keyed begin, terminal or scanner; absence is not key zero. A keyed `attemptQueryBegin` requires the selected observation ref and compares its key with exact managed WRITE/READ and response. All later terminal/scanner/ledgers/accounting must bind that actually observed key. A failed/missing response can retain a completed-WRITE witness while still having no verified method terminal/final. No final is eligible without a complete matched WRITE/READ and exact original-byte closure.
4. **Final boundary remains unchanged:** the inventory selects exactly three ancillary terminal/occurrence/accounting originals plus a distinct attempt manifest, and the final has **exactly 21 immediate `dependency_refs`** with historical predecessor schema bytes unchanged. `accounting_digest` is SHA-256 of complete canonical accounting bytes. The final still requires actual request key; only independent no-replace publication proof and fresh readback can issue typed T/A. Public `PublishReferences` remains fail-closed; lifecycle cutover and private usability have separate decisions.
