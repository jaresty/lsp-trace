# ADR0011 definition terminal V2 — proposed freeze and replay

**REVISE / INCOMPLETE; no terminal freeze or issuance.** This separate proposal does not revise the V1 definition draft, source pins 90/92, production admission, lifecycle D, or any live row. The V2 `$id` and `#/$defs/terminal` are proposed role URIs, not registered authority. The schema checks decoded shape only. No claimant `verified` flag establishes verification.

## Independent selection before production use

Select complete original schema and policy bytes independently, with SHA-256 and length, and the exact role URI. Select the immutable provider profile, executable implementation measurement, query/source/capability/method/evaluation/result originals and privacy/retention policy before invocation; bind their roles, selectors, lengths and SHA-256, not only names or claimant self-hashes. The V1 policy's `framedWireCapBytes:null` is not a positive bound and must not be replaced by an arbitrary number. A positive framed-wire cap may be selected only after Manager-owned observable framed-byte counting and limit precedence have been established and independently tested. Otherwise gate `INCOMPLETE`, including for purported verified terminals. Profile identity and candidate-ID authority also require maintainer selection. No terminal freeze is claimed here.

## Exact original-byte replay predicates

For each preselected role, load exactly its published selector under private no-replace custody; compare raw length and SHA-256 against independently held expected values; compare exact schema role URI and schema/policy original hashes; strictly parse one UTF-8 JSON value, reject duplicate decoded names at every depth, extra fields, noncanonical bytes, trailing values and invalid integer tokens; re-encode by the chosen fixed-field canonical serializer and compare complete bytes including the single LF. The role ref's `bytes` points to the original; a decoded-equivalent reserialization cannot replace it. Verify every predecessor edge and collision domain with role-separated ID `sha256(UTF8(domain) || 0x00 || canonical record bytes excluding id)`; require independently selected domain and canonical fixture before use. Reject self-edges, reused selectors with different bytes, unknown role, cycles, mismatched query/session/generation/request key or raw result digest. The synthetic schema fixtures do not execute these predicates.

For E=P=2 exclusion, require two distinct ordinals 0 and 1, one observed begin and one terminal event for *each*, bounded exact result-payload offsets/digests, `WHOLE_PARSE_SUCCESS`, independently available original-byte target-source evidence for both ordinals including precisely one proven unavailable target, and all-or-none A=0. Counters E=EB=ET=P=2, T=1 only after committed no-replace publication, independent complete selected-original readback and separate fresh **EXTERNAL** final readback. Disposition EXCLUDED; outcome EXCLUDED_VERIFIED. A missing event, unknown denominator, withheld source or failed independent readback prohibits this branch. Exact raw `null` and `[]` have distinct original byte identities and shapes but E=EB=ET=P=A=0; only full parse and external verification permit COMPLETE_EMPTY, EMPTY, T=1. Committed-unverified remains quarantined outcome=null, disposition=null, T=0, A=0 regardless of provisional evaluator observations. Precommit failure has no verified terminal. Neither a self-reported digest nor a matching schema result proves occurrence, source custody, publication, external readback or runtime execution.

## Review stop

The proposed V2 policy explicitly has `framed_wire_cap_bytes:null`: the positive measurable cap and independently selected GO/gopls profile identity are unresolved. A maintainer must choose them from observable implementation/evidence rather than infer them from the decoded-body limit (1,048,576 bytes). Until then this packet is a schema-design proposal only; no accepted freeze, issuance, qualification or authority upgrade. RED/GREEN tests exercise synthetic decoded shapes, not registered originals, production event history or host readback.
