## Established seam

- `censusRuntime.execute` creates and retains the complete typed source plan without discovery.
- Private `censusRuntime.run` currently selects ordinal zero, performs discovery, and creates private constituent terminal custody.
- `privateDiscoveryContinuationTerminal` adapts the exact ordinal-zero terminal into continuation custody.
- `censussourcecontinuation.CoordinateNext` owns one-step CAS, immutable receipts, indeterminate execution, committed prefix, and receipt-linked no-replay resume.
- No current runtime/execute CALLS edge reaches `CoordinateNext`.

## Retained properties

1. Ordinal zero adoption never rediscoveries ordinal zero.
2. One invocation discovers at most the exact next selected partition.
3. Receipt-linked uncertain execution resumes to COMMITTED from the exact authoritative head.
4. Receipt-linked execution is neither rediscovered nor republished.
5. Only COMPLETE_NONEMPTY and SUCCESSFUL_EMPTY advance.
6. FAILED, REFUSED, PARTIAL, and UNKNOWN do not advance.
7. Public completion/envelope bytes remain identical.
8. Legacy/non-private behavior remains identical.
9. No public schema or Program C change.
10. No aggregate graph, cross-partition CALLS, feature identity, acceptance, or authority escalation.
