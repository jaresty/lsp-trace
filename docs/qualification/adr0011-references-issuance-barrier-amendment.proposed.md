# ADR 0011 — proposed references issuance-barrier amendment

**Status: PROPOSED / NOT ACCEPTED FOR IMPLEMENTATION / NOT QUALIFIED.** This is a narrow proposed amendment to the independently accepted `REFERENCES_SYMBOL_V1` implementation contract recorded in `adr0011-references-contract-implementation-acceptance.md` (`ed91b585`; contract SHA-256 `d71ec03c9244eb70fe9d93023410e0dc5cedc9932c992b7ff1ef45f2fc42f7f9`; matrix SHA-256 `c370fd6ae76bb3b74a15da12a7a9c41d9730c68662830294a50dfe89177546a2`). It does not revise those accepted bytes in place. An independent maintainer must explicitly accept or reject this delta before implementation adopts its new record meanings. The historical omitted-selector `CALLS_ONLY` path and all other ADR 0011 gates remain unchanged.

## Contradiction to resolve

The test-owned checkpoint currently computes `T=1,A=P` from complete-result evaluation and publishes an immutable terminal before an occurrence ledger. On occurrence publication or readback failure, this leaves terminal bytes claiming admission even though matrix rows 35–36 require no newly **issued** terminal (`T=0`) or occurrence (`A=0`). Its publication helper also loses the exact selector/digest on committed verification failure. There is **no reciprocal selector/digest cycle** in the current records: the terminal names method/target; the occurrence ledger names the terminal. The problem is the issuance boundary, not a hash cycle. A persisted but unverified artifact is never an issued receipt.

## Proposed acyclic roles and authority

1. A separately replay-verified subordinate document-symbol target and a matched, independently verified references method observation remain prerequisites. Manager-owned request write/read observations are not evaluator events. They do not authenticate a producer.
2. A private, immutable **evaluation proposal** binds the exact target/method receipts, keyed complete raw-result digest, policy, independently recorded ordinal begin/terminal evaluation events, observed `B`, countable `E`, `E_B/E_T`, successful whole-result `P`, and proposed closed outcome/disposition. It has no issued `T` or `A`; malformed results still have whole-result `P=0`, with any diagnostic prefix segregated. Neither parsed-item length nor a self-consistent proposal alone measures `B` or element transitions.
3. A private, immutable **inert occurrence candidate** binds the verified evaluation proposal, method and target, every original ordinal/range/identity and proposed cardinality. It has **no admitted `A`**, is never an eligible typed input, and cannot be replay-promoted by a caller. `null` and `[]` remain distinct retained raw results even when both propose `P=0`. A malformed, limited, or withheld result cannot produce an admission candidate; its mandatory diagnostic arm remains separate.
4. A private, immutable **final issuance receipt** refers backward to the exact verified candidate and proposal selectors/digests. Independent replay checks their expected identities, ordinal multiplicity, method/target source and revision custody, policies, canonical bytes, and all bounded counts before the final receipt is verified. Only after its no-replace publication **and verified readback** may the owner return a composite issuing receipt with one terminal member (`T=1`) and newly admitted occurrences (`A=P`, or zero for eligible `COMPLETE_EMPTY`). The final receipt is the issued terminal authority. Each admitted occurrence binds its proposal, method and target directly and the issued terminal by the verified final→candidate composition; **this indirect terminal binding is an explicit proposed change requiring acceptance**. No public/root-selected issuance follows from this internal test-owned path.
5. Replay of an issued occurrence must start with the exact final receipt and traverse final→candidate→proposal→method/target, with independent expectations at every boundary. It cannot treat an inert candidate, proposal, or failed final publication as a terminal or occurrence receipt. In particular, a substituted or absent final receipt invalidates the entire candidate and every ordinal despite intact method, target and ranges. Occurrence identity must include the original ordinal and transaction-bound target/method identity; candidate selection by caller-supplied selector alone cannot confer admission. Retention transfers from transaction ownership to the admitted ledger only after final verification, with an immutable exact dependency list. Failed/intermediate objects remain private and inactive under a specified bounded transaction-retention policy; deletion, privacy revocation, dependent tombstones and unexplained missing bytes keep the accepted fail-closed lifecycle semantics.

The distinct canonical versions, exact record fields, occurrence identity formula, schema and policy bytes/digests, private retention period/access policy, receipt selector layout, and independently recorded evaluator event source/transition contract remain to be specified and pinned **before this delta can be accepted for implementation**. A replay of counters generated by the same parser without an independently evidenced event source does not satisfy that contract. This draft does not select these details by implication.

## Failure and diagnostic invariant

For **every publication or replay failure in this otherwise valid, fully evaluated success arm** before final verified readback—including method, proposal, candidate, or final precommit failure; committed-but-unverified bytes; missing or substituted replay dependencies—there is **no new issued request outcome, terminal or admitted occurrence**: issued `T=0,A=0`. Evaluation may already have observed `B=1`, `E=2,E_B=2,E_T=2,P=2` in the two-valid-element publication-failure controls, but those counts remain provisional and are not a newly issued ledger. Apply matrix rows 35–36 to failures at **each** success-arm publication stage, not only the first attempt. A committed verification failure must report `PUBLICATION_UNVERIFIED`, retain the exact potentially committed selector/digest in a separately bounded, root-bound **redacted quarantine inventory**, and never retry, delete, or retroactively upgrade that receipt. A precommit failure reports `PRECOMMIT_FAILED` without inventing a committed selector. Failure inventories contain no raw result, source body, or unredacted provider error. The inventory's numeric bound, key/selector custody, owner access, retention and failed-final-readback behavior must be pinned before implementation acceptance.

This success-arm invariant does **not** erase mandatory diagnostic outcomes: independently replayed, bounded and redacted D receipts for keyed server errors or malformed complete results can issue `PROVIDER_FAILURE` or `MALFORMED` and their prescribed terminal counters without admitting occurrences, under the separately selected diagnostic policy. `WITHHELD` cannot silently become a verified diagnostic or an admission. The current failure path publishes an unredacted `ResultBase64` method object before its D record; it must be replaced or kept disabled, not treated as compliant by this amendment.

Persisted bytes that contain a *proposed* outcome or final intended counts but fail readback are inert: only the owner-returned, fully replayed composite receipt defines issued `T/A`. Quarantine is not an admission receipt. A later reviewed successor publication would have a distinct selector/digest and new transaction; it does not validate the original failure retroactively.

## Falsification requirements before independent references qualification

- For equal-location ordinals 0 and 1, and separately for exact `null`/`[]`, assert issued `T=A=0` before and after proposal/candidate readback; only successful final verification returns `T=1,A=2` (or `T=1,A=0` for empty). Check exact ordinal identity and compositional terminal binding. These are test-owned controls until real provider qualification.
- Independently force precommit failure and committed verification failure at method, proposal, candidate, and final publication/readback. Assert no issuing receipt, `T=A=0`, distinct provisional observed counts, exact selector/digest quarantine only for potentially committed bytes, and no retry/retro-upgrade. A corrupted final artifact must never admit an otherwise valid candidate.
- Substitute each proposal/candidate/final selector, digest, ordinal/range, policy/source/revision, count, status and cross-object dependency **one field at a time** with independent expected values. No substituted path issues a new terminal or occurrence. Preserve the original immutable historical bytes and accepted matrix substitution semantics.
- Test privacy withholding, retention dependencies, retirement and revocation tombstones separately. Exact framed-wire/result/Manager body caps and diagnostic redaction remain independent unresolved requirements; this amendment does not count their unexecuted rows as passes.

**Current review disposition: REVISE.** An eventual decision may be `ACCEPT_REFERENCES_ISSUANCE_BARRIER_DELTA_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION | REVISE | REJECT` only after the missing role schemas, policy bytes and event/retention contracts are pinned and the diagnostic arm is reconciled. Acceptance would permit implementation/testing of this narrow delta only, not production issuance, occurrence `QUALIFIED`, definition implementation, grouping or CLI/MCP exposure.
