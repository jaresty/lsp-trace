# Adjudication and acceptance

## Independent semantic challenge

A reviewer or separate review pass challenges the grouping without inheriting its conclusions. Independence is a relationship, not a required person, agent, or tool.

Check for:

- unsupported merge or split conclusions;
- transitive paths treated as direct support;
- union evidence attributed to one seed;
- dispatch, sibling, community, centrality, or instability evidence treated as CALLS or feature semantics;
- successful bounded traversal treated as source completeness;
- bounded zero, `UNKNOWN`, failed, or successful-empty states treated as absence;
- capture-set composition treated as cross-capture CALLS or native custody;
- technical structure promoted into identity, purpose, value, production use, confidence, coverage, or acceptance;
- missing alternatives, falsifiers, or correction lineage;
- untouched ambiguity silently resolved.

Preserve supported disagreement. A challenge can narrow, split, withdraw, or leave a hypothesis unresolved; it cannot manufacture missing authority.

## Stakeholder correction

Product/domain stakeholders may admit semantic propositions under an explicit external authority. Record who/what supplied the correction, its scope, date/revision, affected candidate IDs, predecessors, and finite `inventory_state_delta`. Keep the underlying technical evidence unchanged.

Corrections may rename, merge, split, exclude, or retain candidates. Do not erase prior revisions or unresolved alternatives. A stakeholder label does not retroactively authenticate source, execution, or technical coverage.

If stakeholder authority is unavailable or disputed, set `EXTERNAL_ADJUDICATION` or `UNRESOLVED`; do not guess. Untouched ambiguity remains explicit in the next revision.

## Accepted-inventory boundary

An inventory is accepted only when a named external acceptance contract defines:

- authorized decision-maker or admitted source;
- accepted scope and revision;
- proposition types being accepted;
- treatment of unresolved and excluded candidates;
- correction/reopening procedure;
- explicit coverage ceiling;
- acceptance record linked to candidate revisions.

Technical tools never satisfy this contract on their own. `ACCEPT_BOUNDARY` means a known evidence/scope boundary was consciously retained; it is not accepted feature identity. If no acceptance contract exists, report the inventory as provisional or unresolved.

## Iterate under explicit bounds

Acquire more evidence only for a named unresolved question with a proposed method/seed, expected discriminating result, explicit resource bounds, authority ceiling, and stop condition. Retain each round.

After two consecutive rounds yield no new admissible evidence reference, changed disposition, narrowed alternative, or justified acquisition, the review may stop as `BOUNDED_REVIEW_DRY`. This is a workflow terminal, not complete feature or source coverage.

## Human review view

Prefer one row per candidate:

```text
ID | Working label | Status | Seeds | Evidence | Alternatives | Gaps | Revision
```

Link to details containing exact references, failures, boundaries, competing interpretations, corrections, and acceptance authority. Lead with provisional capability and a public-safe explanation; make exact records and receipts optional audit detail.

Attach examples and relationships only through exact stable joins and a stated bounded selection rule. If no exact relationship is established, say `not established`. Broader product/source evidence stays at its broader scope.

Navigation groups, ordering, layout, proximity, color, communities, and visual density do not alter identity or status. Keep ungrouped candidates visible. Zero assignments to a group is an assignment state, not evidence of no capabilities.

Place unresolved merge/split questions next to affected candidates with evidence for each interpretation, blocking unknowns, and correction-safe consequences. Presentation improves comprehension only; any semantic regrouping requires an explicit revision and lineage.
