# LOCAL_QUALIFICATION_PRIVACY_V1 — proposed local-only privacy profile

**PROPOSED / NOT SELECTED / NOT IMPLEMENTED.** This profile is only for opt-in, single-user local development and qualification. It does **not** authorize hosted, shared, remotely accessible, or automatically CI-retained evidence. Those modes require a separate operational policy. It does not change the accepted ADR 0011 matrix, producer authentication, or final-issuance replay requirements.

## Three modes

1. **Normal local use (default):** process provider results in memory; retain no exact raw result; issue bounded redacted diagnostics only. No raw bytes in CLI/MCP responses, public selectors, logs, exception text or committed artifacts. When an exact malformed result cannot be independently replayed, classify the corresponding claim `WITHHELD` or `INCOMPLETE`; never manufacture a verified D or issued references occurrence. Matched server-error code disclosure, including zero, is denied by default; without a separately selected explicit authorization, use `WITHHELD`, not a code-erased replayable D.
2. **Synthetic qualification (preferred durable evidence):** self-authored fixtures with no copied real source, personal paths, credentials or provider secrets may be committed and replayed exactly. Boundary and redaction canaries are synthetic. Such fixtures prove only their tested behavior, not a real provider's identity, custody, completeness or the unexecuted matrix rows.
3. **Optional live-provider investigation:** exact raw capture requires explicit **per-run** local opt-in; it is never inferred from a passed selector or automatically enabled by a test. The user selects a local root before capture; require it to be outside tracked workspaces and known synced/automatically backed-up locations, and validate owner-local 0700 directory, 0600 single-link no-replace regular files and exact readback. Same-user access suffices; no organization roster. Do not print or expose the root/path, raw bytes or private selector through public CLI/MCP, logs, diagnostics, errors, quarantine metadata or committed artifacts. No automatic backup or CI artifact upload. Reject known synced/automatically backed-up roots; for an unknown backup/sync status, require explicit per-run user confirmation that the selected root will not be automatically backed up or uploaded. Capture must be tied to the owner-observed keyed response, not caller-provided bytes.

## Bounded local capture proposal

The initial candidate bounds are **1,048,576 exact result bytes/object; at most 16 objects and 16 MiB per run; at most 128 MiB of private raw objects under the selected root**. These are review candidates, not accepted runtime measurements. Count before copy and no-replace publication, account even potentially committed objects, reject on exhaustion, and never silently evict or truncate. The independent Manager, staged-frame, framed-wire, message, work, allocation and deadline bounds remain separate; raising Manager `MaxBytes` alone currently breaks owned-pair eligibility. Raw objects are optional for malformed diagnostic replay, not a substitute for the final issuance transaction's verified dependency list.

## Retention and issuance distinction

Live raw debugging retention is **user-managed**: manual deletion is sufficient and needs no enterprise ACL roster, backup administrator or privacy-revocation tombstone. After deletion, exact replay is unavailable and any affected qualification row returns to `INCOMPLETE`, unless a separately replayable sanitized synthetic fixture independently establishes that claim. The harness must check availability at each replay and may not continue asserting replayability from a stale digest. It must not delete bytes automatically to make room.

**Separate mandatory issuance integrity:** a proposal/candidate/final transaction still needs bounded accounting, exact no-replace selector/digest readback, and fail-closed `T=A=0` before verified final issuance. A committed-but-unverified receipt is never promoted, retried or silently reinterpreted. The accepted lifecycle for any actually issued immutable receipt is not relaxed by the optional debug-file deletion rule; missing required final dependencies make new replay/admission unavailable. Normal local use and diagnostic-only synthetic tests do not issue a final success-arm receipt by default.

## Review boundary

A maintainer must independently select/pin this versioned profile and its SHA-256, confirm the per-run opt-in and owner-bound capture mechanism, and test restricted permissions, storage caps, deletion-to-`INCOMPLETE`, no auto-backup, and canary absence from every public/log/error sink. Shape-valid private-raw manifests alone do not establish capture authority or replay. The historical `CALLS_ONLY` default remains unchanged. Production `REFERENCES_SYMBOL_V1` qualification and shared-service privacy policy remain separate later decisions.
